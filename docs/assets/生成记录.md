Beyond the group, progressively simplify into broad pale sage and cream watercolor washes, two or three soft tree silhouettes and spacious softly lit air; very little mark-making in the upper half, mostly light warm paper and diluted color. Make this feel intentionally edited, spacious and readable at small sizes, not a uniformly detailed miniature landscape. Strongest contrast and sharpest edges only at the friends' faces and nearby basket; softer fewer edges elsewhere. No additional animals or characters. No text, no title, no numerals, no lettering, no logos, no borders, no watermark, no UI or labels; the website overlays the label separately. No Disney redesign, no red shirt, no striped sweater. Opaque background.
